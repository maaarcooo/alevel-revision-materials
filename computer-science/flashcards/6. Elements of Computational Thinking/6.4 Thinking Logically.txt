What is a decision in problem solving? | A result reached after some consideration
What does thinking logically allow a programmer to do? | Plan for different scenarios, by foreseeing the decisions made throughout the whole program
Why should decision points be identified early when building a solution? | So that enough information about the options can be gathered to make an informed decision
How can decision making be simplified when designing a solution? | By first limiting the options to those that are feasible and suited to the problem
What should be considered when choosing a programming language for a solution? | Its suitability and functionality for the problem, and how familiar or quick to learn it is in the time available
What is a flowchart? | A diagram of the flow of a program that shows every point where a decision has to be taken
How is a decision shown in a flowchart? | As a diamond with two outcomes, yes or no (true or false)
Name three ways to plan an algorithm before coding it | Flowcharts, pseudocode and structured English
Why are flowcharts useful for identifying decision points? | They show the flow of control visually, so decisions are clear and easy to follow
What is the main disadvantage of flowcharts? | They are time-consuming to create
What advantage does pseudocode have for planning an algorithm? | It mimics the constructs of programming languages without the problem of syntax
What makes a piece of pseudocode acceptable? | Its meaning is clear, since pseudocode has no fixed syntax
How does structured English compare with pseudocode? | It is usually more verbose and less precise
Why is an algorithm planned before it is written in a particular language? | Languages differ in syntax, constructs and idiosyncrasies, so a plan can be implemented in any of them
Which three constructs do structured programming techniques use? | Sequence, selection and iteration
What is the purpose of structured programming techniques? | To aid the readability, understanding and maintainability of code
What is a block-structured language? | One that uses only sequence, selection and iteration to control the flow of execution, such as Python
Why should each block of code have a single entry and exit point? | To avoid unintended flow of control, such as leaving a subroutine early or branching unintentionally
In which two kinds of structure do decisions occur in a program? | Selection (if statements, switch/case) and iteration (usually while loops)
By what other name is selection known? | Branching
How does a selection structure use a decision? | A Boolean condition directs the flow of control down one of several paths
How does an iteration structure use a decision? | A Boolean stopping condition determines whether the instructions are repeated
Which three factors are weighed when choosing between options for a solution? | Effectiveness, convenience and feasibility
How are effectiveness, convenience and feasibility used to reach a decision? | They are ordered from most to least important, and the option that best meets the top priority is chosen
Why might the priority given to effectiveness, convenience and feasibility differ between projects? | It depends on the purpose of the software and its end-users
What are logical conditions? | Conditions, depending on one or more variables, that determine the next step whenever a system has to make a decision
Conditions, depending on one or more variables, that determine the next step whenever a system has to make a decision - what term is this? | Logical conditions
What is a Boolean condition? | A condition that evaluates to either true or false
Where do most errors in a program occur? | In evaluating Boolean conditions, especially long conditions with multiple clauses
Which two kinds of operator are combined to build Boolean conditions? | Comparison operators (such as ==, != and >=) and logical operators (AND, OR, NOT and XOR)
How does XOR differ from OR in a Boolean condition? | OR is true when either or both conditions are true. XOR is true only when exactly one of them is true
A cinema admits someone only if age >= 15 AND money >= 8. Why is AND the correct operator? | Both conditions must be true for entry. With OR, meeting just one of them would be enough
What do decisions affect in a program? | The flow of control, which is the route taken through the program
How does a decision made by the user affect a program? | The program follows a different route for each choice, so the outcome of every choice must be planned
What is the effect of nesting selection and iteration statements? | Each inner statement adds another decision point that further affects the flow of the program
How does a switch/case statement compare with a chain of if/elseif statements? | They are functionally identical. Each case or branch is a decision point leading to different statements
Why should break instructions be used with care? | They change the flow of control abruptly, so misuse can cause unintended problems
