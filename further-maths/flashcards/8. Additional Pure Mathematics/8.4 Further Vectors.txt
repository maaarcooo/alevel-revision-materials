What is the geometric definition of the vector product $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$? | $\lvert \mathbf{a} \rvert \lvert \mathbf{b} \rvert \sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{n}}$, where $\mathbf{a}$, $\mathbf{b}$ and $\hat{\mathbf{n}}$ form a right-handed triple
In which direction does $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$ point? | Perpendicular to both $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, the way a right-handed screw moves when turned from $\mathbf{a}$ to $\mathbf{b}$
What is $\mathbf{i} \times \mathbf{j}$? | $\mathbf{k}$, and in turn $\mathbf{j} \times \mathbf{k} = \mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{k} \times \mathbf{i} = \mathbf{j}$
What is $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{a}$? | $\mathbf{0}$, since the angle between the vectors is 0
What is the distributive property of the vector product? | $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{c}$
How is it shown that the vector product is not associative? | Find one example where $\mathbf{p} \times (\mathbf{q} \times \mathbf{r}) \neq (\mathbf{p} \times \mathbf{q}) \times \mathbf{r}$
Which of $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ and $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ is a scalar? | $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$, the scalar triple product. The other is a vector
Why must $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ be of the form $\lambda\mathbf{b} + \mu\mathbf{c}$? | $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}$ is perpendicular to the plane of $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$, and $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ is perpendicular to $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}$, so it is parallel to that plane
How is $(\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) \times \mathbf{a}$ written in terms of $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$? | $-\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$, by anti-commutativity
Why does $\mathbf{x} \times \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{0}$ for non-zero $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$ mean they are parallel? | $\lvert \mathbf{x} \rvert \lvert \mathbf{y} \rvert \sin\theta = 0$ with neither modulus 0, so $\sin\theta = 0$ and $\theta = 0$ or $\pi$
Besides parallel vectors, when else is $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0}$? | When $\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{0}$ or $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0}$
How is a parameter found that makes two vectors parallel? | Find it from one pair of components, then check it works for all three components
What does the equation $(\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{a}) \times \mathbf{d} = \mathbf{0}$ describe? | The line through the point $A$ in the direction $\mathbf{d}$
Why can $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{a} + t\mathbf{d}$ be written as $(\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{a}) \times \mathbf{d} = \mathbf{0}$? | $\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{a} = t\mathbf{d}$ is parallel to $\mathbf{d}$, so its vector product with $\mathbf{d}$ is $\mathbf{0}$
What does $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c} = \mathbf{a}$ show about the line $OA$? | It is perpendicular to the plane containing $O$, $B$ and $C$
What is the area of a parallelogram with sides $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$? | $\lvert \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} \rvert$
What is the area of triangle $ABC$ using the vector product? | $\frac{1}{2}\lvert \overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} \rvert$
What is the area of triangle $OAB$ with $\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}$? | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$, so the area is $\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{21}$
How is the perpendicular distance from $C$ to the line $AB$ found with the vector product? | $\frac{\lvert \overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} \rvert}{\lvert \overrightarrow{AB} \rvert}$, the parallelogram's area divided by its base
How is the value of $t$ found that minimises the area of a triangle whose vertices depend on $t$? | Minimise $\lvert \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} \rvert^2$ as a function of $t$, which avoids the square root
How is $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ calculated? | Work out $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}$, then its scalar product with $\mathbf{a}$, or find the determinant with rows $\mathbf{a}$, $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$
What is $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ for $\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$, $\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{c} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$? | $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$, so $1 + 4 - 6 = -1$
How can the vectors in $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ be reordered without changing its value? | Cyclically, as $\mathbf{b} \cdot (\mathbf{c} \times \mathbf{a})$ or $\mathbf{c} \cdot (\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b})$, while swapping two vectors changes the sign
What is the volume of a parallelepiped with edges $\mathbf{a}$, $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$ from one vertex? | $\lvert \mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) \rvert$
What is the volume of the tetrahedron $PQRS$? | $\frac{1}{6}\lvert \overrightarrow{PQ} \cdot (\overrightarrow{PR} \times \overrightarrow{PS}) \rvert$
What does $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = 0$ show? | The three vectors are coplanar, so $O$, $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie in one plane
If the tetrahedron $OABC$ has a fixed volume but $C$ is unknown, where can $C$ lie? | On one of two planes parallel to $OAB$, one on each side, since $\mathbf{c} \cdot (\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b})$ can be positive or negative
How is the distance from $S$ to the plane $PQR$ found from the tetrahedron's volume $V$ and the area $A$ of triangle $PQR$? | $h = \frac{3V}{A}$, from $V = \frac{1}{3}Ah$
