What is the gradient of the line through $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$? | $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$
What is the equation of the line with gradient $m$ through $(x_1, y_1)$? | $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$
What is the equation of the line with gradient 2 through $(1, 3)$? | $y - 3 = 2(x - 1)$, so $y = 2x + 1$
What is the midpoint of $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$? | $\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$
What is the distance between $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$? | $\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$
What is the distance between $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 6)$? | $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$
What is the condition for two lines with gradients $m_1$ and $m_2$ to be parallel? | $m_1 = m_2$
What is the condition for two lines with gradients $m_1$ and $m_2$ to be perpendicular? | $m_1 m_2 = -1$
What is the gradient of a line perpendicular to a line with gradient $\frac{3}{4}$? | $-\frac{4}{3}$
How is the point of intersection of two lines found? | Solve their equations simultaneously
What is the equation of the circle with centre $(a, b)$ and radius $r$? | $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2$
How are the centre and radius found from an equation such as $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 12 = 0$? | Complete the square in $x$ and in $y$
What are the centre and radius of $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 12 = 0$? | $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25$, so centre $(3, -2)$ and radius 5
After completing the square, when does $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = k$ represent a circle? | When $k > 0$
If $PQ$ is a diameter of a circle, how are the centre and radius found? | The centre is the midpoint of $PQ$ and the radius is half the length of $PQ$
What is the angle in a semicircle? | A right angle
What does the perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord do? | It bisects the chord
How is a tangent to a circle related to the radius at the point of contact? | They are perpendicular
How is the equation of the tangent to a circle at a given point found? | Find the gradient of the radius to that point, take its negative reciprocal, then use $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$
How can the centre of a circle through three given points be found? | Intersect the perpendicular bisectors of two of the chords
How is it found whether a line meets a circle? | Substitute the line into the circle equation and find the discriminant of the resulting quadratic
What does the discriminant of the quadratic from a line and a circle show? | $> 0$: two intersections, $= 0$: the line is a tangent, $< 0$: they do not meet
How is it found whether two circles intersect? | Compare the distance between their centres with the sum and the difference of their radii
What is a parametric equation of a curve? | An equation giving $x$ and $y$ each in terms of a third variable, the parameter
How is a pair of parametric equations converted to a cartesian equation? | Eliminate the parameter, by making it the subject of one equation or by using an identity
What is the cartesian equation of the curve $x = 2t$, $y = t^2$? | $t = \frac{x}{2}$, so $y = \frac{x^2}{4}$
Which identity eliminates $t$ from $x = a\cos t$, $y = b\sin t$? | $\sin^2 t + \cos^2 t = 1$, giving $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$
Why does using $\arcsin$ to eliminate the parameter usually not give the whole curve? | $\arcsin$ gives only principal values, so part of the curve is lost
