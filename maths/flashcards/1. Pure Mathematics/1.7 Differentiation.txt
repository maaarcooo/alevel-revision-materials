What does $\frac{dy}{dx}$ represent? | The gradient of the tangent to the curve, the rate of change of $y$ with respect to $x$
What is the definition of the derivative from first principles? | $f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}$
When differentiating from first principles, why must $-f(x)$ be written in brackets? | Every term of $f(x)$ must be subtracted, or signs go wrong
What is the final step of differentiation from first principles? | Let $h \to 0$, written with the limit notation, once the numerator has been simplified and divided by $h$
What is the derivative of $x^2$ from first principles? | $\frac{(x + h)^2 - x^2}{h} = \frac{2xh + h^2}{h} = 2x + h \to 2x$ as $h \to 0$
What is the derivative of $x^n$? | $nx^{n - 1}$

What is the chain rule? | $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du} \times \frac{du}{dx}$

What is the condition for a stationary point? | $\frac{dy}{dx} = 0$

What does $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} < 0$ at a stationary point show? | A maximum

What does the second derivative measure? | The rate of change of the gradient
When is a function increasing on an interval? | When $f'(x) > 0$ throughout the interval
