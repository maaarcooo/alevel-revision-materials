What is diffraction? | The spreading out of waves after they pass through a narrow gap or around an obstruction
The spreading out of waves after they pass through a narrow gap or around an obstruction — what term is this? | Diffraction
When are diffraction effects most noticeable? | When the wavelength of the wave and the width of the gap are similar in size
What produces the bright fringes in a single slit diffraction pattern? | Constructive interference of each part of the wavefront as it passes through the slit
What produces the dark fringes in a single slit diffraction pattern? | Destructive interference of each part of the wavefront as it passes through the slit
Describe the central maximum in a single slit diffraction pattern compared to the subsidiary maxima | It is much wider and brighter than the other bright fringes
How do the subsidiary maxima change as the order increases in a single slit diffraction pattern? | They become narrower and dimmer
In the single slit intensity pattern, what is the intensity at the dark fringes? | Zero
How does the central maximum of a single slit pattern compare in width to a double slit central maximum? | The single slit central maximum is much wider
Why is the central maximum bright white when white light is diffracted through a single slit? | Constructive interference from all wavelengths (colours) occurs at the central position
What are the non-central maxima composed of when white light is diffracted through a single slit? | A spectrum of colours
In white light single slit diffraction, which colour appears nearest to the central maximum? | Violet/blue
In white light single slit diffraction, which colour appears furthest from the central maximum? | Red
Explain why blue light appears closer to the central maximum than red light in white light single slit diffraction | Shorter wavelengths are diffracted less, so they spread out less and appear nearer the centre
How does fringe spacing change further from the central maximum in white light single slit diffraction? | It gets smaller, and spectra eventually merge without any space between them
How does the proportion of red and blue wavelengths change at higher-order maxima in white light single slit diffraction? | The amount of red wavelength increases and the amount of blue wavelength decreases
How does the intensity of the central maximum compare between white light and monochromatic light in single slit diffraction? | They are equal in intensity
How do the non-central maxima of white light compare to those of monochromatic light in single slit diffraction? | They are wider and less intense
How does the double slit interference pattern differ from the single slit diffraction pattern? | Double slit has equally spaced maxima of equal intensity. Single slit has a distinctive central maximum with subsequent maxima at decreasing intensity
What is the single slit 'envelope' in a double slit experiment? | The single slit diffraction pattern that modulates the double slit interference fringes, causing their intensity to decrease away from the centre
How does increasing the gap size (relative to wavelength) affect diffraction? | The waves spread out less after passing through the gap
How does increasing the wavelength affect the single slit diffraction pattern? | The angle of diffraction increases, producing wider fringes
How does making the slit narrower affect the angle of diffraction? | The angle of diffraction increases
How does making the slit narrower affect the width of the central maximum? | The central maximum becomes wider
How does making the slit narrower affect the intensity of the maxima? | The intensity decreases
How does making the slit narrower affect fringe spacing? | The spacing between fringes becomes wider
What property of a wave stays constant when it is diffracted? | The wavelength (distance between wavefronts). Only the amplitude changes
What is a diffraction grating? | A piece of optical equipment consisting of a large number of very thin, equally spaced parallel slits carved into a glass plate
How does a diffraction grating pattern compare to a double slit pattern? | The bright fringes are narrower and brighter, while the dark regions are wider and darker
What does a diffraction grating produce when monochromatic light passes through it? | A pattern of bright and dark fringes
What does a diffraction grating produce when white light passes through it? | It separates the white light into its different wavelength components (spectra)
What is the diffraction grating equation? | $d \sin \theta = n\lambda$
In the diffraction grating equation $d \sin \theta = n\lambda$, what does $d$ represent? | The distance between adjacent slits on the grating (m)
In the diffraction grating equation $d \sin \theta = n\lambda$, what does $\theta$ represent? | The angle of diffraction of the light of order $n$ from the normal as it leaves the grating
In the diffraction grating equation $d \sin \theta = n\lambda$, what does $n$ represent? | The order of the maxima (number of maxima away from the central maximum, where $n = 0$)
How is the slit spacing $d$ calculated from the number of lines per unit length $N$? | $d = \frac{1}{N}$
If a diffraction grating has 300 lines per mm, what is the slit spacing in metres? | $d = \frac{1}{300} = 3.33 \times 10^{-3}$ mm $= 3.33 \times 10^{-6}$ m
How is the angular separation between two orders of maxima found? | By subtracting the smaller angle from the larger one
What is the maximum angle of diffraction at which maxima can be observed from a diffraction grating? | $\theta = 90°$ (when $\sin \theta = 1$)
How is the highest visible order of maxima calculated for a diffraction grating? | $n = \frac{d}{\lambda}$, rounded down to the nearest integer
Why must the calculated value of $n = \frac{d}{\lambda}$ be rounded down? | Because $n$ must be an integer number of maxima, and $\sin \theta$ cannot exceed 1
What is the path difference between light from adjacent slits at the $n$th order maximum of a diffraction grating? | $n\lambda$
What is the path difference at the zeroth-order (central) maximum of a diffraction grating? | Zero
How is the diffraction grating equation derived from the path difference? | At the $n$th order maximum the path difference is $n\lambda$. From trigonometry, $\sin \theta = \frac{n\lambda}{d}$, which rearranges to $d \sin \theta = n\lambda$
Why are diffraction gratings used in spectrometers? | They separate light of different wavelengths with high resolution, enabling measurement of wavelength, stellar composition, and red shift
Why can a thin crystal act as a diffraction grating for X-rays? | The wavelength of X-rays is similar in size to the gaps between atoms in the crystal
What can the diffraction pattern from X-ray crystallography be used to measure? | The atomic spacing in materials
