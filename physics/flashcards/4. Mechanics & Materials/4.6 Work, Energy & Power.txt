What is work done by a force equivalent to? | A transfer of energy
What is the unit of work done? | Joule (J), equivalent to 1 N m
Define mechanical work | The amount of energy transferred when an external force causes an object to move over a certain distance
The amount of energy transferred when an external force causes an object to move over a certain distance -- what term is this? | Mechanical work (work done)
What does the work done by a resultant force on a system equal? | The change in energy in that system
What is the equation for work done by a constant force parallel to the displacement? | $W = Fs$, where $F$ = average force (N) and $s$ = displacement (m)
What is the equation for work done when the force is at angle $\theta$ to the displacement? | $W = Fs\cos\theta$
In the equation $W = Fs\cos\theta$, what does $\theta$ represent? | The angle between the direction of the force and the direction of motion of the object
When calculating work done with a force at an angle, which component of the force is used? | The component parallel to the displacement
What is power? | The rate of doing work, or the rate of energy transfer
The rate of doing work or the rate of energy transfer -- what quantity is this? | Power
What is the equation relating power, work done, and time? | $P = \frac{\Delta W}{\Delta t}$, where $P$ = power (W), $\Delta W$ = work done (J), $\Delta t$ = time (s)
What is the unit of power? | Watt (W)
What is the equation for power in terms of force and velocity? | $P = Fv$, where $F$ = force (N) and $v$ = velocity (m s$^{-1}$)
Under what conditions can the equation $P = Fv$ be used? | When an object moves at constant velocity with a constant force in the direction of the velocity
What does the area under a force-displacement graph represent? | The work done
How is work done found when the force is not constant? | From the area under the force-displacement graph (splitting into standard shapes if necessary)
Why can the equations $W = Fs$ and $P = Fv$ not be used for a variable force? | Because the force is not constant; work done must instead be found from the area under the force-displacement graph
What happens to an object's acceleration when a varying force acting on it increases? | The acceleration increases
Define efficiency | The ratio of the useful power output from a system to its total power input
The ratio of the useful power output from a system to its total power input -- what quantity is this? | Efficiency
What is the equation for efficiency in terms of power? | $\text{Efficiency} = \frac{\text{useful power output}}{\text{total power input}} \times 100\%$
What is the equation for efficiency in terms of energy? | $\text{Efficiency} = \frac{\text{useful energy output}}{\text{total energy input}} \times 100\%$
In what two formats can efficiency be expressed? | As a ratio (between 0 and 1) or as a percentage (between 0% and 100%)
What are the units of efficiency? | Efficiency has no units, because it is a ratio of two quantities with the same units
What determines whether a type of energy transfer is useful or wasted in a system? | The purpose of the system (e.g. in a lightbulb light is useful and heat is wasted, whereas in a heater heat is useful and sound is wasted)
State the principle of conservation of energy | Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it can only be transferred from one form to another
What does the principle of conservation of energy imply for a closed system? | The total amount of energy remains constant, although the amount in each form may change
What is dissipated energy? | Energy transferred to the surroundings in forms that cannot easily be used for a useful purpose (wasted energy)
In what forms is energy commonly dissipated? | Thermal (heat), light, and sound energy
What energy transfer occurs for a falling object in a vacuum? | Gravitational potential energy is converted to kinetic energy
What energy transfer occurs for a horizontal mass oscillating on a spring? | Elastic potential energy is converted to kinetic energy and vice versa
For an object travelling up a rough inclined surface, state the energy conservation equation | Loss in kinetic energy = gain in gravitational potential energy + work done against friction
What is kinetic energy? | The energy an object has due to its motion (velocity)
What is the equation for kinetic energy? | $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
In the kinetic energy equation, which quantity is squared? | Only the speed ($v$), not the mass or the $\frac{1}{2}$
How should the loss of kinetic energy be expressed in a calculation? | As a positive value, because energy is a scalar quantity
What is gravitational potential energy? | Energy stored in a mass due to its position in a gravitational field
What is the equation for the change in gravitational potential energy in a uniform field? | $\Delta E_p = mg\Delta h$, where $m$ = mass (kg), $g$ = gravitational field strength (N kg$^{-1}$), $\Delta h$ = change in height (m)
Under what condition is $\Delta E_p = mg\Delta h$ valid? | In a uniform gravitational field (such as near the Earth's surface)
What happens to gravitational potential energy when a mass is lifted? | It increases (the mass gains GPE)
What happens to gravitational potential energy when a mass falls? | It decreases (the mass loses GPE, which is transferred to kinetic energy)
What is the relationship between gravitational potential energy and height in a uniform field? | Linear -- a graph of GPE against height is a straight line
What is the equation for elastic potential energy? | $E_{el} = \frac{1}{2}k(\Delta L)^2$
