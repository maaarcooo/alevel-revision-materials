What is the Young Modulus? | The ratio of tensile stress to tensile strain ($E = \frac{FL}{A\Delta L}$), measuring the stiffness of a material
The ratio of tensile stress to tensile strain, measuring the stiffness of a material — what quantity is this? | The Young Modulus
What are the units of the Young Modulus? | Pascals (Pa)
A wire of length 2.0 m and cross-sectional area $1.0 \times 10^{-7}\;\mathrm{m^2}$ extends by 1.0 mm under a force of 10 N. What is its Young modulus? | $E = \frac{FL}{A\Delta L} = \frac{10 \times 2.0}{1.0 \times 10^{-7} \times 1.0 \times 10^{-3}} = 2.0 \times 10^{11}$ Pa
How does the Young modulus differ from the spring constant? | The Young modulus is a property of the material, whatever its dimensions. The spring constant belongs to one object and depends on its length and cross-sectional area
Explain why the Young Modulus has the same units as stress | Strain is dimensionless, so dividing stress by strain gives units of stress
What is the typical order of magnitude of the Young modulus of a metal? | About $10^{11}$ Pa (around 100 GPa)
How is the Young Modulus determined from a stress-strain graph? | It equals the gradient of the linear region, where Hooke's Law is obeyed
How is a stiff material recognised on a stress-strain graph? | By a steep gradient in the linear region, which shows a high Young modulus
How does finding the Young Modulus differ between a stress-strain graph and a force-extension graph? | On a stress-strain graph the gradient gives the Young Modulus directly, whereas on a force-extension graph the gradient must be multiplied by $\frac{L}{A}$
What is the aim of the Young Modulus required practical? | To measure the Young Modulus of a metal in the form of a wire
In the Young Modulus experiment, what is the independent variable? | Force (or load) in N
In the Young Modulus experiment, what is the dependent variable? | Extension in m
In the Young Modulus experiment, what are the control variables? | Original length of wire, thickness of wire, and the metal used
Why is a long, thin wire used in the Young modulus experiment? | It gives a larger extension for each load, which reduces the percentage uncertainty in the extension
Why is the diameter of the wire measured with a micrometer and not a ruler? | A micrometer has a resolution of 0.01 mm, so the percentage uncertainty in the small diameter is much lower than with a ruler (1 mm)
How should the diameter of the wire be measured to reduce the effect of random error? | Take readings at different points along the wire and in different orientations, reject any anomalies and calculate the mean
How is the cross-sectional area of the wire calculated from its diameter? | $A = \frac{\pi d^2}{4}$
What is the role of the pulley in the Young Modulus experiment? | It allows the mass to hang vertically, introducing less friction than the edge of the table
What is the purpose of the tape marker in the Young Modulus experiment? | To mark a reference point on the wire so the extension can be accurately read from the ruler after each load is added
What graph is plotted to determine the Young Modulus from experimental data? | Force (load) on the y-axis against extension on the x-axis
How is the Young Modulus calculated from a force-extension graph? | Young Modulus $= \text{gradient} \times \frac{L}{A}$, where $L$ is the original length and $A$ is the cross-sectional area
In the Young Modulus experiment, how is the load calculated from the mass? | $F = mg$, where $g = 9.81 \text{ N kg}^{-1}$
How should the wire be checked for exceeding its elastic limit during the experiment? | Remove the load and check the wire returns to its original length before taking new readings
What random error affects reading the marker on the ruler in the Young Modulus experiment? | Parallax error
How can random errors in the extension measurements be reduced? | Repeat the experiment for all loads and find an average extension
What improvement reduces systematic error when measuring extension in the Young Modulus experiment? | Use a vernier scale instead of a metre ruler for more precise extension readings
What safety precautions are taken in the Young Modulus experiment? | Wear safety goggles in case the wire snaps, and place a cushion below the mass hanger in case it falls
