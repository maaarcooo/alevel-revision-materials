Why is an object moving in a circle at constant speed accelerating? | Its direction of motion, and so its velocity, is continuously changing
In what direction is the velocity of an object in circular motion at any instant? | Along the tangent to the circle
What is centripetal acceleration? | The acceleration of an object moving in a circle, directed towards the centre of the circle
The acceleration of an object moving in a circle, directed towards the centre of the circle - what term is this? | Centripetal acceleration
What is centripetal force? | The resultant force towards the centre of the circle that keeps an object moving in a circular path
The resultant force towards the centre of the circle that keeps an object moving in a circular path - what term is this? | Centripetal force
In what direction does the centripetal force act relative to an object's velocity? | Perpendicular to it
Why are the centripetal force and the centripetal acceleration in the same direction? | By Newton's second law, an object accelerates in the direction of the resultant force on it
What kind of force is a centripetal force? | Not a separate force. It is the resultant of the real forces acting, such as tension, friction or gravity
What provides the centripetal force on a car turning on a level road? | Friction between the tyres and the road
What path does an object in circular motion follow if the centripetal force is suddenly removed? | A straight line along the tangent to the circle
Explain why a centripetal force does no work on an object in uniform circular motion | The force is always perpendicular to the direction of motion, so the object never moves in the direction of the force
What happens to the speed of an object acted on only by a centripetal force? | It stays constant. Only the direction of motion changes
What is a radian? | The angle subtended at the centre of a circle by an arc equal in length to the radius
The angle subtended at the centre of a circle by an arc equal in length to the radius - what unit is this? | The radian (rad)
How many radians are there in a complete circle? | $2\pi$
How is an angle converted from degrees to radians? | Multiply it by $\frac{\pi}{180}$
What is $90^\circ$ in radians? | $90 \times \frac{\pi}{180} = \frac{\pi}{2}\;\text{rad}$
What is an angle in radians in terms of the arc length $s$ and the radius $r$? | $\theta = \frac{s}{r}$
What is angular displacement? | The angle, in radians, through which an object has turned about the centre of its circle
What is angular speed? | The angle turned through per unit time, $\omega = \frac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t}$
The angle turned through per unit time - what quantity is this? | Angular speed, $\omega$
What is the unit of angular speed? | $\text{rad s}^{-1}$
What is the equation for angular speed in terms of linear speed and radius? | $\omega = \frac{v}{r}$
What is the equation for angular speed in terms of frequency and period? | $\omega = 2\pi f = \frac{2\pi}{T}$
What is the angular speed of a wheel rotating at 5.0 Hz? | $\omega = 2\pi f = 2\pi \times 5.0 = 31\;\text{rad s}^{-1}$
What is the linear speed of a point 0.40 m from the axis of a wheel turning at $12\;\text{rad s}^{-1}$? | $v = \omega r = 12 \times 0.40 = 4.8\;\text{m s}^{-1}$
For points on the same rigid rotating object, how do angular speed and linear speed vary with distance from the axis? | Angular speed is the same for every point. Linear speed is proportional to the distance from the axis
What is the equation for centripetal acceleration in terms of linear speed? | $a = \frac{v^2}{r}$
What is the equation for centripetal acceleration in terms of angular speed? | $a = \omega^2 r$
What is the centripetal acceleration of a car travelling at $20\;\text{m s}^{-1}$ round a bend of radius 50 m? | $a = \frac{v^2}{r} = \frac{20^2}{50} = 8.0\;\text{m s}^{-2}$
What is the centripetal acceleration of a point 1.5 m from the axis of an object rotating at $3.5\;\text{rad s}^{-1}$? | $a = \omega^2 r = 3.5^2 \times 1.5 = 18\;\text{m s}^{-2}$
What is the equation for centripetal force in terms of linear speed? | $F = \frac{mv^2}{r}$
What is the equation for centripetal force in terms of angular speed? | $F = m\omega^2 r$
What centripetal force keeps a 0.30 kg ball moving at $4.0\;\text{m s}^{-1}$ in a circle of radius 0.80 m? | $F = \frac{mv^2}{r} = \frac{0.30 \times 4.0^2}{0.80} = 6.0\;\text{N}$
What centripetal force acts on a 2.0 kg mass moving in a circle of radius 0.50 m at $6.0\;\text{rad s}^{-1}$? | $F = m\omega^2 r = 2.0 \times 6.0^2 \times 0.50 = 36\;\text{N}$
How does the centripetal force needed change when the speed doubles at the same radius? | It increases by a factor of 4, since $F \propto v^2$
For a mass $m$ on a string at the bottom of a vertical circle, how are the tension $T$ and the weight related to the centripetal force? | $T - mg = \frac{mv^2}{r}$
For a mass $m$ on a string at the top of a vertical circle, how are the tension $T$ and the weight related to the centripetal force? | $T + mg = \frac{mv^2}{r}$
At which point of a vertical circle is the tension in the string greatest, for a mass moving at constant speed? | At the bottom, where the tension supports the weight as well as supplying the centripetal force
