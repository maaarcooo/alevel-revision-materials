What is electromagnetic induction? | The process by which an emf is induced in a conductor when the magnetic flux linkage through it changes
Which three quantities can change to change the flux linkage through a coil? | The flux density, the coil's area, or the coil's angle to the field
What is magnetic flux? | The product of magnetic flux density and the area perpendicular to the field: $\Phi = BA$
The product of magnetic flux density and the area perpendicular to the field — what quantity is this? | Magnetic flux
What is the unit of magnetic flux? | Weber (Wb)
What is one weber in terms of the tesla? | $1\ \text{Wb} = 1\ \text{T m}^2$
What is the magnetic flux through an area of $0.050\ \text{m}^2$ perpendicular to a uniform field of $0.20\ \text{T}$? | $\Phi = BA = 0.20 \times 0.050 = 0.010\ \text{Wb}$
What is magnetic flux linkage? | The product of the magnetic flux through a coil and its number of turns, $N\Phi$
The product of the magnetic flux through a coil and its number of turns — what quantity is this? | Magnetic flux linkage
What is the unit of magnetic flux linkage? | Weber turns (Wb turns)
What is the flux linkage of a coil of $N$ turns and area $A$ at an angle to a uniform field $B$? | $N\Phi = BAN\cos\theta$
In $N\Phi = BAN\cos\theta$, what angle is $\theta$? | The angle between the field lines and the normal to the plane of the coil
What is the flux linkage of a 100-turn coil of area $0.020\ \text{m}^2$ in a $0.50\ \text{T}$ field with its normal at $60^\circ$ to the field? | $N\Phi = BAN\cos\theta = 0.50 \times 0.020 \times 100 \times \cos 60^\circ = 0.50\ \text{Wb turns}$
When is the flux through a flat coil in a uniform magnetic field at its maximum? | When the plane of the coil is perpendicular to the field lines ($\theta = 0^\circ$)
When is the flux through a flat coil in a uniform magnetic field zero? | When the plane of the coil is parallel to the field lines ($\theta = 90^\circ$)
State Faraday's law | The magnitude of the induced emf is equal to the rate of change of flux linkage
State Lenz's law | The induced emf acts in a direction that produces effects opposing the change that causes it
What is the equation for the magnitude of the emf induced in a coil of $N$ turns? | $\varepsilon = N\frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}$
What does the minus sign in $\varepsilon = -N\frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}$ represent? | Lenz's law (it gives the direction of the induced emf)
What is the magnitude of the emf induced in a 50-turn coil when the flux through it changes by $2.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{Wb}$ in $0.10\ \text{s}$? | $\varepsilon = N\frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} = 50 \times \frac{2.0 \times 10^{-3}}{0.10} = 1.0\ \text{V}$
What does the gradient of a flux linkage against time graph represent? | The induced emf
What apparatus is used to verify Lenz's law? | A bar magnet, a coil of wire and a sensitive ammeter (no cell)
When the north pole of a bar magnet is pushed into a coil, what pole is induced at the end of the coil nearest the magnet? | A north pole, which repels the magnet
When the north pole of a bar magnet is pulled out of a coil, what pole is induced at the end of the coil nearest the magnet? | A south pole, which attracts the magnet
Using the right-hand grip rule on a coil, what do the curled fingers and the thumb show? | Fingers: the direction of the current. Thumb: the direction of the magnetic field inside the coil (towards its north pole)
Why is no emf induced when a magnet is held stationary inside a coil? | The flux linkage is not changing
How does the induced emf change when a magnet is pulled out of a coil rather than pushed in? | Its direction reverses
Name three changes that increase the emf induced by a magnet moving through a coil | Move the magnet faster, use more turns, use a stronger magnet
What is the frequency of the emf induced in a coil placed in the magnetic field of a second coil connected to an a.c. supply? | The same as the supply frequency
Which device uses electromagnetic induction to convert kinetic energy into electrical energy? | A generator
What emf is induced across a straight conductor of length $L$ moving at speed $v$ perpendicular to a uniform field of flux density $B$? | $\varepsilon = BLv$
In which direction must a straight conductor move through a magnetic field for the induced emf to be maximum? | Perpendicular to the field lines
Explain how $\varepsilon = BLv$ follows from Faraday's law for a rod moving perpendicular to a uniform field | In time $\Delta t$ the rod sweeps an area $Lv\Delta t$, cutting flux $\Delta\Phi = BLv\Delta t$. Dividing by $\Delta t$ with $N = 1$ gives $\varepsilon = BLv$
What is the emf across a $0.25\ \text{m}$ rod moving at $3.0\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ perpendicular to a $0.40\ \text{T}$ field? | $\varepsilon = BLv = 0.40 \times 0.25 \times 3.0 = 0.30\ \text{V}$
What length is used for $L$ when calculating the emf between the wing tips of an aircraft flying through Earth's magnetic field? | The wingspan
What is the flux linkage of a coil rotating at angular speed $\omega$ in a uniform field, timed from when its plane is perpendicular to the field? | $N\Phi = BAN\cos(\omega t)$
What emf is induced in a coil rotating at angular speed $\omega$ in a uniform field, timed from when its plane is perpendicular to the field? | $\varepsilon = BAN\omega\sin(\omega t)$
What is the peak emf of a 200-turn coil of area $0.010\ \text{m}^2$ rotating at $50\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$ in a $0.10\ \text{T}$ field? | $\varepsilon_0 = BAN\omega = 0.10 \times 0.010 \times 200 \times 50 = 10\ \text{V}$
For a coil rotating in a uniform magnetic field, at what orientation is the induced emf maximum? | When the plane of the coil is parallel to the field lines
For a coil rotating in a uniform magnetic field, at what orientation is the induced emf zero? | When the plane of the coil is perpendicular to the field lines
Explain why the emf of a coil rotating in a uniform field is greatest when its flux linkage is zero | At that orientation the coil's sides cut the field lines fastest, so the flux linkage changes at its greatest rate
What is the phase difference between the flux linkage and the induced emf of a coil rotating in a uniform field? | $90^\circ$
Which calculator mode is needed to evaluate $\sin(\omega t)$ when $\omega$ is in $\text{rad s}^{-1}$? | Radians
What is the aim of the search coil required practical? | To find how the flux linkage through a search coil varies with the angle between the coil and the magnetic field
How is the magnetic field produced in the search coil practical? | A slinky spring (large coil) connected to a low-voltage 50 Hz a.c. supply
Why is an a.c. supply used for the slinky in the search coil practical? | It produces a changing magnetic field, so an emf is induced in the stationary search coil
What instrument measures the induced emf in the search coil practical? | A cathode-ray oscilloscope (CRO)
What is the independent variable in the search coil practical? | The angle $\theta$ between the normal to the search coil and the field lines
What is the dependent variable in the search coil practical? | The peak induced emf
Which two properties of the search coil are controlled in the search coil practical? | Its area and its number of turns
Which two properties of the slinky's magnetic field are kept constant in the search coil practical? | Its peak flux density and its frequency
Why is the CRO time-base switched off in the search coil practical? | So the trace is a vertical line whose length shows the peak-to-peak voltage
How is the peak emf found from the CRO trace in the search coil practical? | Halve the peak-to-peak voltage
Where along the slinky is the search coil placed in the search coil practical? | Halfway along it
What is the starting orientation of the search coil in the search coil practical? | Its plane perpendicular to the field, so $\theta = 0^\circ$
How is the angle varied in the search coil practical? | The coil is rotated in $10^\circ$ steps up to $90^\circ$, measured with a protractor
What graph is plotted in the search coil practical? | Peak emf $\varepsilon_0$ against $\cos\theta$
What shape is the expected graph of peak emf against $\cos\theta$ in the search coil practical? | A straight line through the origin
What is the gradient of the graph of peak emf against $\cos\theta$ in the search coil practical? | $B_0AN\omega$
What relationship between flux linkage and angle does the search coil practical confirm? | Flux linkage (and so peak emf) is proportional to $\cos\theta$
Why might the graph of peak emf against $\cos\theta$ have a non-zero intercept at $\cos\theta = 0$? | The field lines are not perfectly uniform, so some flux still links the coil at $90^\circ$
How is parallax error reduced when reading the protractor in the search coil practical? | Read it from directly above, at the same point each time
How could the reliability of the search coil practical be improved? | Take readings over a full $360^\circ$ rotation
How could the angle increments be set more precisely in the search coil practical? | Rotate the coil with a calibrated motor in fixed increments
How is the protractor kept in the same place between readings in the search coil practical? | Fix it with blu-tack
Why should the supply to the slinky not be left on longer than necessary? | The coil heats up, especially if its wire is thin
What limit must not be exceeded to avoid damaging the slinky coil? | Its rated current
What checks on the leads reduce the risk of a short circuit or fire in the search coil practical? | No damaged wires or connections, and appropriate fuses fitted
